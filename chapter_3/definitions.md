## Classifiers

**Support vector machine (SVM)** = A classifier that finds the decision boundary with the widest possible margin between the classes. It relies only on the training instances closest to that boundary, called support vectors.

**Support vector classifier (SVC)** = The classification form of an SVM. In scikit-learn it is implemented as `SVC` in `sklearn.svm`.
